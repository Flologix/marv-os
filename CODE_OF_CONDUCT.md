# Code of conduct

Marv OS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without their permission are not accepted
anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fk@flologixai.com**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainers
may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may ban anyone from the project for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report those privately as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
